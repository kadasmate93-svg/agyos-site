AGY_OS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how AGY_OS accesses, uses, stores and shares data when its owner or another explicitly authorized user connects a Google account or uses an enabled AGY_OS feature.

AGY_OS is a private, self-hosted personal and family automation system. It is not offered as a public advertising, analytics or data-broker service.

2. Google data AGY_OS may access

Depending on the features enabled by the user and the permissions granted during Google OAuth authorization, AGY_OS may access:

  • Gmail messages, message metadata, labels and related information needed for enabled mail automations;
  • Google Calendar events and related scheduling information;
  • Google Drive and Google Sheets data explicitly used by configured AGY_OS workflows;
  • basic Google account information required to identify the authorized account.

AGY_OS does not intentionally access Google user data beyond permissions granted by the user and required by configured features.

3. How Google data is used

Google user data is used only to provide user-requested personal and family automation features, including organizing information, detecting relevant notifications, producing summaries, coordinating schedules and updating configured personal records.

AGY_OS does not sell Google user data, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to build advertising profiles.

4. Storage and security

AGY_OS is primarily self-hosted. Authorized data and authentication credentials may be stored on systems controlled by the owner when required for enabled functionality. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to restrict access to authorized users and services.

OAuth credentials and tokens are not intentionally published on this website or in public source repositories.

5. Sharing

AGY_OS does not sell or rent personal information. Google user data is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide a user-requested function, when the user explicitly directs such sharing, or when required by law.

Personal or family notifications may be sent to destinations explicitly configured by the user, such as private messaging or notification services.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed for configured personal workflows or until the owner removes it. Users can revoke AGY_OS access to their Google account through their Google Account security settings. Local AGY_OS data can also be deleted by the system owner.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

AGY_OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated when AGY_OS functionality or data practices change. The current version will remain published at this location with an updated revision date.
