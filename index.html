AGY_OS

Personal & Family Automation

AGY_OS is a private, self-hosted system that helps its owner and family organize everyday information and automate routine tasks.

What AGY_OS does

AGY_OS connects selected services used by the owner and explicitly authorized family members and turns authorized information into useful personal workflows.

  • Processes selected Gmail messages and metadata for personal automations such as package, order and notification handling.
  • Works with Google Calendar for personal and family scheduling workflows.
  • Uses Google Sheets and related Drive data where explicitly configured for household and personal organization.
  • Provides private notifications and summaries to authorized users.

Why Google data is requested

AGY_OS requests access only to Google data needed for features that the user enables and only after the Google account owner grants permission. Google user data is used to provide those requested personal automation features.

Google user data is not sold and is not used for advertising.

See the Privacy Policy for details about access, use, storage, sharing and deletion.

Availability

AGY_OS is not a public SaaS product. It is a private personal and family system operated for a small set of explicitly authorized users.
